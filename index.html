Straight answers

Questions you should ask us.

No agency fog. The economics and expectations are agreed before we build anything.

How can we trust this will work?

You don’t have to take it on faith. We agree on the current baseline, the success metric, and the measurement window before we start. If the metric doesn’t improve, you pay OrcBrain $0.

How expensive are you?

Our income is tied to your outcome. We agree on a percentage of the verified additional revenue or savings before we build. For time savings, we also agree on its dollar value upfront. No improvement means no success fee.

How long does this take?

Honestly: it depends on the workflow. A focused automation can move quickly; a complicated system takes longer. After the first call, we’ll give you a clear scope and turnaround estimate—and prioritize the shortest path to a measurable result.

How much of our time will this take?

As little as we can reasonably ask for. We need one clear walkthrough of the bottleneck, access to the relevant tools, and quick decisions when something affects your process. We handle the implementation, testing, and follow-through without asking you to babysit us.

What should we automate first?

The expensive, repetitive bottleneck with a clean metric: missed-lead follow-up, estimate turnaround, scheduling, manual data entry, status updates, or reporting. If we can’t isolate a measurable outcome, we’ll tell you it isn’t a good pilot.

Will this replace our team or current software?

Usually, no. The goal is to remove repetitive work around the systems your team already uses—not force a risky software migration. We only recommend a new tool when the expected payoff justifies the disruption.
