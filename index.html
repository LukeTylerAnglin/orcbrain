COMMON QUESTIONS

Clear terms from the start.

Is the pilot actually no-cost?+

Yes. There is no setup fee for the pilot. You only consider paid ongoing work if the pilot meets the operating metric agreed in advance.

What counts as success?+

We set it together before build starts: for example, response speed, completed follow-through, fewer manual touches, or documented team capacity reclaimed.

No. The first pilot is designed around the tools and habits your team already relies on wherever practical.

No. Your team remains in control. The pilot focuses on reducing repetitive work and making useful information easier to act on.

Questions

Before the pilot

What do you need from us?

Nothing before the first call. In a pilot, we use exports or screen share for appointments/procedures, payments/discounts, treatment costs, capacity, and promotion activity. We reconcile messy reports when needed.

Does the queue change as the schedule changes?

Yes—on the refresh cadence the available data supports. The pilot is managed and schedule-aware, usually refreshed weekly at first. We do not call it real-time unless a practice's data access genuinely supports that.

Is this new software for the team?

No. OrcBrain is a remote, managed decision loop using the systems you already have. We build software only when a paid client needs a repeatable workflow.

Do you guarantee a profit increase?

No. We guarantee the agreed decision loop. Financial impact depends on the practice’s data, owner approval, implementation, and a controlled measurement process.
